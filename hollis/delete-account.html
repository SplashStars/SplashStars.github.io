Delete your Hollis data

It is all on your phone, so you can do it yourself in ten seconds.

HomePrivacyDelete your data

There is no Hollis account and no cloud copy, so there is nothing to ask us to delete. Everything Hollis holds about you is in one encrypted database on your phone, and both ways below destroy it.

In the app

  1. Open Hollis.
  2. Open the menu and go to Settings.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete all my data.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Every task, reminder, alarm, event, bill, subscription, warranty, document and item is erased, and the database is destroyed with them. It cannot be undone, which is why Hollis offers to export your data first — deleting does not have to mean losing it.

By uninstalling

Uninstalling Hollis removes the app's storage, and the database with it. Nothing of yours is left behind on the phone and nothing of yours was ever anywhere else.

What we could not delete even if you asked

Held byWhat, and what to do about it
Google Play Your purchase record, if you subscribed. That is a payment record and Google keeps it independently of us — we never see it and cannot remove it. You can see it in your Play account.
Your phone Any export file you shared somewhere yourself, and any photo that was already in your gallery before you attached it. Those are yours to delete where they sit.

If you only want to stop paying

That is a different thing. Cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store → Subscriptions. Hollis keeps working, free, with a banner on list screens. Your data is untouched.

Still want to write to us?

If something above did not work, or you want it confirmed in writing, email esellersstores@gmail.com and we will answer. We cannot delete anything on your behalf, because we have no access to your phone — but we can help you do it.
