Hollis keeps your life admin on your phone. There is no account to make, no server holding your bills, and no analytics watching you use the app. Nothing you type into Hollis is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Everything below explains the exceptions: the few things that do leave the device, what they are, and how to avoid them.

What Hollis stores on your phone

Your tasks, reminders, alarms, events, bills, subscriptions, warranties, documents and the things you own are written to a database on the device. That database is encrypted, and the key is held in the Android keystore, so another app cannot read it and it is not readable if the phone's storage is copied.

The Insights screen is calculated on the phone from the entries you already made. No figures are uploaded to produce it.

Photos and PDFs you add to Documents stay on the phone. Text is read out of them by on-device recognition — the image is never sent anywhere to be read.

We never receive any of it. We operate no server for Hollis and hold no copy of your entries, your documents or your email address.

What leaves the phone

1. Advertising only after the free period, and only on the free plan

Hollis is free for the first three months with no ads at all. After that you either subscribe, or keep using Hollis free with a banner on list screens. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which reads your device's advertising ID to choose them and to measure them. That ID identifies the device, not you, and it is the only identifier anything in Hollis sends anywhere.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and subscribing removes ads — and therefore this — completely. Nothing you wrote in Hollis is sent to AdMob, or used to choose an ad, ever.

Google's handling of ad requests is covered by Google's own policy. In the EU and the UK Hollis asks for your consent before the first ad request, through Google's consent form, and honours the answer.

2. Voice capture only while you hold the button

If you speak to Hollis instead of typing, Android's own speech recognition turns your words into text. Depending on your phone and its settings, that may be done by Google on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy. Hollis receives only the resulting text and keeps it on the phone. Typing instead avoids this entirely.

3. Payments

The subscription is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Hollis never sees or stores your card details — the app is told only whether a subscription is active.

4. Exports you share yourself

Settings → Your data → Export all my data writes a file and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice — Drive, email, a cable. Hollis does not upload it anywhere on its own.

What Hollis never does

  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud copy of your entries.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your entries with anyone, for any purpose.
  • No reading your contacts, call log, SMS, location or other apps' data.
  • No advertising profile built from what you wrote in Hollis.

How long things are kept

On the phone, until you delete them or uninstall the app. There is no other copy. See Delete your data.

Your choices

  • Export everything. Settings → Your data → Export all my data. A file you keep.
  • Delete everything. Settings → Your data → Delete all my data, or uninstall Hollis.
  • Remove ads. Subscribe, and no advertising ID is read again.
  • Use Hollis with no account at all. This is the only way it works, and always will be.

Children

Hollis is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you in-app before the change takes effect.
